# 📍 Gurugram Startup & Job Market Map

An interactive corporate ecosystem map and job market tracker for **Gurugram (Millennium City)**. Built as a single-page web application to help job seekers, investors, and engineers discover local companies by office location, funding stage, and industry sector.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Leaflet Map**: Visualizes company office locations across Gurugram with sector-coded markers.
- **Granular Filtering**: Filter companies by:
  - **Areas**: DLF Cyber City, Udyog Vihar, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, Sector 44 & 32, SPR, Manesar, etc.
  - **Stages**: Bootstrapped, Seed, Series A/B, Series C+, Unicorn, Public/Enterprise, and GCCs.
  - **Sectors**: Fintech, Consumer Tech, Autotech, Healthtech, Foodtech, D2C, Deeptech, Real Estate Tech, etc.
- **Dual View Modes**: Switch seamlessly between **Map View** and a responsive **Card/List Grid View**.
- **Real-Time Search**: Search companies dynamically by name, region, stage, or industry.
- **Direct Navigation Links**: Quick-access buttons for company websites, career portals, and official LinkedIn profiles.
- **Ecosystem Ticker**: Live status counter for Unicorns, GCCs, and Enterprise hubs.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5 & CSS3**: Custom dark-mode design system with flex/grid layouts.
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)**: Lightweight filtering engine and state management.
- **Leaflet.js**: Mapping library powered by CARTO Dark Matter map tiles.
- **Google Fonts**: Space Grotesk, Inter, and JetBrains Mono.
